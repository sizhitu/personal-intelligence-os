# Prompt: translate Chinese reflection subtitles into natural English

You are translating a personal Chinese spoken reflection into clear, natural English subtitles.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Preserve the speaker's meaning, intent, nuance, and personal voice. Do not add claims, examples, conclusions, or advice.
2. Do not rewrite or improve the Chinese. The Chinese subtitle is the source of truth.
3. Translate for natural spoken English, not literal word-for-word equivalence.
4. Keep the same SRT cue count, cue numbers, and exact timestamps. Do not merge, split, reorder, renumber, or retime cues.
5. Keep each English cue concise enough to read at the same time as the Chinese cue. Prefer natural sentence fragments over overly compressed or awkward wording.
6. Preserve names, numbers, dates, tickers, and technical terms. If a term is ambiguous, flag it after the SRT rather than guessing.
7. Return only the complete English SRT in the first code block. After the code block, list any genuine ambiguities briefly. Do not include commentary inside the SRT.

## Tone

Thoughtful, calm, intelligent, conversational, and sincere. The result should sound like a fluent person expressing an idea, not a formal essay or machine translation.
