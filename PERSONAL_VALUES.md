# Personal Values and Preference Model

This is a schema/template, not a claim that every field is already known.

Each item should record:
- value
- source: explicit_user_statement | user_confirmed_inference | inferred | imported
- confidence: high | medium | low
- created_at and optional review_after
- sensitivity: ordinary | personal | sensitive
- consent_scope when another person's data is involved

Explicitly stated values outrank inferred preferences. Inferences remain hypotheses until confirmed.

## Profile dimensions
- Values and non-negotiable commitments
- Goals and desired outcomes
- Constraints: time, money, location, skills, obligations
- Risk tolerance and acceptable downside
- Time horizon
- Decision style and preferred detail
- Communication preferences
- Domain-specific preferences

## Rules
- Do not infer sensitive traits.
- Do not turn temporary states into permanent preferences.
- Ask for confirmation when an inference would materially change a recommendation.
- Preserve conflicting preferences with context and dates.
- Provide ways to inspect, correct, export, and delete profile data.
