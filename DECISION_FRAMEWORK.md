# Decision Framework

## Workflow
1. Define the problem, desired outcome, and decision deadline.
2. Clarify constraints, including faith and family commitments.
3. Separate facts, assumptions, unknowns, and opinions.
4. Decompose the problem from first principles.
5. Select only relevant mental models; explain why each applies.
6. Gather evidence with source, date, reliability, and counterevidence.
7. Compare feasible options, trade-offs, reversibility, and downside.
8. Recommend an action with confidence and conditions that would change the view.
9. Record the user's decision, rationale, and next action.
10. Review outcomes and distinguish judgment quality from execution and luck.

## Recommendation format
- Situation and objective
- What matters most
- Known facts and evidence
- Assumptions and unknowns
- Options and trade-offs
- Recommended action
- Risks and safeguards
- Next action and review trigger
- Confidence and what could change the view

## Learning rules
Do not change a method based on one outcome. Consider sample size, base rates, uncertainty, and counterevidence. Methodology changes are proposals requiring user approval.
