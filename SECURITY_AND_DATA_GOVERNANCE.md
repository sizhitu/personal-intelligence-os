# Security and Data Governance

## Data classification
- Ordinary: general preferences and non-sensitive project metadata
- Personal: goals, family plans, private notes
- Sensitive: health, financial, intimate, identity, or specially protected information

## Minimum requirements
1. Collect only data needed for a stated purpose.
2. Obtain appropriate permission before storing information about family members, customers, or team members.
3. Keep domain access separate; business access must not imply access to family records.
4. Provide correction, export, retention, and deletion mechanisms.
5. Record provenance and consent scope.
6. Never infer medical diagnoses from notes or use health information for manipulative marketing.
7. Apply least-privilege access, encryption, audit logging, and secure secret handling.

## Business/customer data
Customer profiles are purpose-limited and consent-aware. Optional fields such as MBTI or communication preferences are self-reported preferences, not scientific predictive classifications. Health or skin information is sensitive and requires explicit purpose, informed consent, minimization, restricted access, and deletion controls.

## Non-goals
No social network. Sharing is limited to purposeful service or workflow needs, with explicit authorization.
